Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing SferaLuna and for the detailed feedback. We would like to respectfully request reconsideration of the 4.3(b) determination, and to clarify what distinguishes SferaLuna from generic dating apps in the category.

**SferaLuna is the native companion to an established, live service.** This is not a speculative template submitted to test a saturated market. SferaLuna is a fully operational product already running in production on the web (sferaluna.com), with a real, active user base. The iOS app is the mobile extension of that existing service — the same accounts, the same community, the same backend — not a standalone clone.

**Identity verification is core to the product, not an afterthought.** Unlike the majority of apps in this category, SferaLuna requires real identity verification through Stripe Identity (KYC). This is central to our value proposition: a safer, authenticated environment. Combating fake profiles and catfishing is a structural design decision, and it materially changes the user experience compared to open-signup dating apps.

**The app is built around community and safety features that go well beyond swiping.** SferaLuna includes several features not found together in typical dating apps:

- **Circle of Six** — a personal safety network letting members share their whereabouts and plans with trusted contacts.
- **VibeSphere, VibeMentor, and VibePlanner** — a mood-based community feed, a peer Q&A space, and a collaborative outing planner. These make SferaLuna a social and community platform, not only a matching tool.
- **Luna Events** — curated in-person and community events.
- **Ghost Mode** — advanced privacy controls letting members remain invisible while browsing.

**We serve a deliberately focused niche.** SferaLuna is a French-language, premium service designed around the female experience, safety, and authenticity, aimed at women aged 28 and over. This intentionally narrow positioning — in language, audience, tone, and safety focus — differentiates the app from the broad, general-purpose dating apps the guideline refers to.

Taken together, the verified-identity requirement, the safety-first community architecture, and the focused French-language premium positioning give SferaLuna a distinct experience that is not already available on the App Store.

We would welcome the opportunity to walk a reviewer through these features directly, and would be glad to schedule a call if that would help clarify how the app differs from others in the category. Please let us know if any additional information or demonstration would be useful.

Thank you again for your time and consideration.

Best regards,
The SferaLuna Team
