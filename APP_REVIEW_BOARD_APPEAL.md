Dear App Review Board,

We are appealing the rejection of SferaLuna (Submission ID: adaf971c-8eee-40aa-b80f-7e7bb97b8fc9) under Guideline 4.3(b). We submitted a detailed clarification through the Resolution Center, but the response we received did not address the specific points we raised and simply invited us to resubmit. We are therefore respectfully asking the Board to re-evaluate the decision on its merits.

We believe the 4.3(b) determination rests on the impression that SferaLuna is a generic, template-style dating app. It is not, and we would like to set out concretely why.

**1. SferaLuna is the native extension of an existing, operational service — not a speculative clone.**
SferaLuna is already live in production on the web at sferaluna.com, with a real, active user base. The iOS app shares the same accounts, the same community, and the same backend. This is not a template submitted to test a saturated market; it is the mobile client of a service that already exists and is in active use.

**2. Mandatory identity verification is a structural differentiator.**
Unlike most apps in the category, SferaLuna requires real identity verification through Stripe Identity (KYC) before members can participate. This is not a cosmetic feature — it is foundational to the product's purpose of providing a safer, authenticated environment, and it directly changes the user experience compared with open-signup dating apps.

**3. The app is built around community and personal-safety features that go well beyond matching.**
- **Circle of Six** — a personal safety network allowing members to share their whereabouts and plans with trusted contacts.
- **VibeSphere, VibeMentor, and VibePlanner** — a mood-based community feed, a peer-to-peer Q&A space, and a collaborative outing planner, making SferaLuna a social and community platform rather than only a matching tool.
- **Luna Events** — curated community and in-person events.
- **Ghost Mode** — advanced privacy controls letting members browse while remaining invisible.

**4. The product serves a deliberately narrow niche.**
SferaLuna is a French-language, premium service designed around the female experience, safety, and authenticity, targeted at women aged 28 and over. This intentionally focused positioning — in language, audience, tone, and safety emphasis — sets it apart from the broad, general-purpose dating apps that Guideline 4.3 is intended to address.

Taken together, the mandatory identity verification, the safety-first community architecture, and the focused French-language premium positioning constitute a distinct experience that is not already available on the App Store.

We would greatly welcome a phone call with the Board or with App Review to walk through these features directly and answer any questions. We are fully committed to complying with the App Store Guidelines and to demonstrating what makes SferaLuna genuinely different.

Thank you for reconsidering our submission.

Best regards,
The SferaLuna Team
